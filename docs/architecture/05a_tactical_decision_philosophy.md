# Tactical Decision Philosophy

## Intent Over Motion

The AI never decides raw movements.
It decides **intentions**.

Movement is an implementation detail, not a decision.

---

## Why This Matters

Low-level actions provide sparse, noisy rewards.
High-level intentions produce clear, evaluable outcomes.

The system must learn:
- When to advance
- When to attack
- When to reposition

Not:
- How to move one tile up or down

---

## Human Alignment

This mirrors how humans reason tactically:
- “Flank the enemy”
- “Hold this position”
- “Push aggressively”

The AI operates at the same conceptual level.
